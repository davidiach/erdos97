# Discovery record

All results below concern the unchanged nine-point seed. These counts are
execution records, not separate proofs of the geometric reduction.

The preceding coarse two-free probe retained 2,676 of 4,500 support/region
cases. It did not enforce common radii at zero-old-support centers or
simultaneous witness-row compatibility.

The first new exact sweep covered all 4,500 cases using continuous triangle
and slot bounds, zero-old common-radius covers, complete chosen-row search,
and circle-sharing/crossing constraints. With a depth 20 guard, it produced
14,306 closed leaves and 30 unresolved leaves in 18 cases. Those unresolved
leaves were explicitly retained, not counted as exclusions.

The 18 cases were rerun with depth 36. Fifteen closed. Three symmetry-related
cases retained one unresolved subdomain each: cells (2,5) with old witnesses
(3,4), cells (2,8) with (3,5), and cells (5,8) with (4,5). Their geometric domains
approached existing old vertices, but that observation was not used as proof.

The corresponding witness systems were then coupled by their ordinary-
distance equality quotient. Strict Kalmanson zero/inverse tests rejected all
24 distinct terminal closed systems. Every certificate uses actual cyclic
quadruples with four distinct cell ranks. A simple two-inequality example
is given in proof.md. No floating LP verdict or failed numerical realization
is used for the exclusion.

The final partition retains the previous closed paths and closes all three
last subdomains. It contains 14,621 closed leaves covering all 4,500 cases.
The primary and independent mathematical replayers both pass the entire
partition. Repeated replay is validation of the same result, not additional
mathematical advances.

The exact known recurrence through t3 gives the 15-point positive control.
Both new control implementations certify its six rich added vertices,
exactly three internally supported added vertices, and six old good points.
A preliminary control implementation had an unsupported reversed comparison;
it failed before any control result was issued and was corrected. A syntax
error in the cancellation-record helper was also caught before its successful
replay. No conclusion relies on either failed execution.

The exploratory files are separate from the final maintained checkers.
The final validation and file manifest bind the delivered sources and data.
