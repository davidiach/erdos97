# Frontier after this research cycle

## What changed

There is now a concrete distinction between **repairing the old polygon**
and **closing a rich cap**. A general extension lemma repairs any chosen
old vertices. For the exact nine-point seed, nine additions are both
necessary and sufficient when the six original deficient radii are retained.
An explicit off-carrier cap achieves that old-vertex repair.

The new vertices do not inherit richness. More strongly, the 84-triple
certificate proves that each new rich vertex in any convex extension of
that fixed seed needs at least two new witnesses at its selected radius.
A cap made of independently old-supported rich points is unavailable.

The middle-witness forest shortcut is now obstructed for genuinely
four-witness rows, not just for three-witness rows, even when all nine
assigned centers have at most two closer points. The control still has six
nonrich vertices. Global richness remains a logically meaningful extra
hypothesis.

## What did not change

No unconditional proof, all-rich strictly convex counterexample, general
finite-size bound, or accepted repository status transition was obtained.
The new 18-point construction has **nine** good vertices, not fewer than the
six in the starting seed. The sharp lower bound fixes the old coordinates
and their chosen deficient radii. The two-old-witness bound fixes the seed.
Neither excludes moving the old vertices or changing their witness classes.

## Unresolved obligations made concrete

**Construction:** an append-only all-rich cap over this seed must be solved
as a mutually supporting system. At every new vertex, at least two of the
four witnesses must also be new. Changing old coordinates or old radii is
still allowed in a genuine co-design search, but then the fixed-seed
certificates must not be applied beyond their hypotheses.

**Proof:** acyclicity of the entire middle-witness graph is unavailable.
A successful return argument must exploit a stronger global condition than
“the centers on this cycle are rich” or “the assigned radii have at most two
closer points.” The all-rich variable-radius two-closer case and the
unrestricted-radius extraction remain unresolved.

**Numerical search:** a near-distance quartet chosen independently at each
vertex often already violates the exact circle-sharing rules. The delivered
search adds label-valid starting tables and individual row mutations before
constrained optimization. Eight short runs did not find a near-zero
realization. They do not establish infeasibility, and surviving these few
preflight checks does not establish Euclidean realizability.
