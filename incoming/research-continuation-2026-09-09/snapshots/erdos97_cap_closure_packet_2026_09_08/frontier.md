# Research frontier after the cap-closure classification

## Newly established in the written proof and exact checks

For the fixed nine-point seed, an arbitrary finite rich cap whose new
vertices can each use two old witnesses is uniquely the next recurrence
triangle. The proof derives, rather than imposes, the cap size three,
equilateral shape, common center, rotational labeling, radius, and carrier.

The finite reduction is reusable: an old-pair bisector has at most one
admissible slot on each ray, each of capacity one. For n old points, the
corresponding two-old-assigned vertices number at most n(n-1), independently
of richness. In this seed the exact slot count is 42, and a two-outdegree
peeling argument leaves three.

## Consequence for construction

A nontrivial all-rich repair cannot simply make every added vertex rich
with two old witnesses and two new witnesses. At least one new vertex must
obtain at least three witnesses from the added set at every radius that
makes it rich. The fixed-seed theorem does not require the old vertices'
radii to remain unchanged.

The alternatives that remain are materially different: create a cap with
stronger internal equal-distance support, or move the old seed during
co-design. The latter invalidates the fixed-seed slot/circumcenter
certificates and requires recomputation, not extrapolation.

The coarse one-free-vertex interval relaxation survives in all nine old
insertion cells. It is not a geometric candidate and not an exclusion.
Its missing constraints include a common radius at the free center,
simultaneous edge realization, circle-sharing geometry, mutual convexity,
and richness at the old vertices.

## Consequence for proof

This is a local-to-global closure argument within a stated seed-supported
class: every finite cap is covered before any finite graph is searched.
It does not supply a reduction of an arbitrary all-rich polygon to this
seed or to two-old support. It also does not close the variable-radius
two-closer return problem reviewed in the repository.

## What is not new

The next recurrence triangle, the seed's six exceptional vertices, and the
84-triple two-old-witness ceiling were in the preceding packet or repository.
They are replayed here, not renamed as new constructions or new prerequisites.
