## A completed all-size exclusion of the opposite-chain parabola scaffold

The documented two-parabola moment closure is impossible for arbitrary finite
real parameter sets. The potential `g(t)=t^2+c/4` changes sign under the row
average; a maximum of its absolute value forces four distinct witnesses to
have one square. The proof does not require convexity or a radius restriction.
A constant-drift/finite-extremum argument also excludes any deterministic
cycle of parallel parabolic carriers. Mixed-carrier witness rows remain open.

## A completed all-size obstruction to a precise proof relaxation

For every k>=4, the new rational metric construction simultaneously satisfies
all of the following: at least k unit witnesses at every vertex; distinct
nonunit distances within each row; strict triangle and Kalmanson inequalities;
all Ptolemy inequalities, including witness-circle equalities; all-radius
pair-fiber overlap at most one; and exact strict-convex planar realizability
of each center with its entire rich fiber in the induced cyclic order.

The whole metric is nevertheless not planar Euclidean. The different rich
stars are separately realizable but not mutually compatible. This blocks that
specific relaxation, not all geometric proof strategies. Cross-center
relations and true planar distance/angle compatibility remain available.

The explicit 138-label instance has independently represented exact local
charts and exhaustive integer checks. It also admits positive scalar turns
satisfying every equal-distance interval-turn inequality from the repository.
Those are not actual angles compatible with all distances; the proof note
explicitly demonstrates that limitation. The arbitrary-k theorem does not
claim an arbitrary-k turn-vector extension.

The first metric control, retained separately, makes even all Ptolemy
inequalities strict but does not preserve the local witness-circle geometry.

## A completed restricted geometric theorem

With at most sixteen vertices, three consecutive nonempty blocks cannot make
every vertex four-rich using witnesses exclusively outside its own block.
A source-row packing bound reduces to block sizes at most five; pair-budget
saturation then forces a five-cycle to be an inversion graph. This is not a
new bound for arbitrary counterexamples.
