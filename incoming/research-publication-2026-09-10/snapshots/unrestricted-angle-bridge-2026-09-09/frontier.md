and at most two shared witnesses give 2,266 types after anonymous-label and
reflection reduction (the two centers remain distinguished). Necessary

All 912 contradiction certificates and all 1,354 relaxation assignments have

562 of these contradictions pass the implemented basic two-circle/crossing
and vertex-circle nesting tests. This is a comparison with these particular

## Construction escape and results

Higher Danzer products supply more than two optional cross-factor witnesses
per orbit. Deleting product orbits changes the incidence system and may remove
the complete diamonds used in the fixed-27 proof. This explicitly escapes
that proof's labelled-system hypothesis, but not arbitrary other obstructions.

All 300 recorded floating-point full-pool hulls have empty named-witness
2-cores. This does not exclude other convex subsets of those pools, which may
retain points interior to the full pool. Alternative-subset search is the
next active construction experiment. Neither float hulls nor MILP termination
are exact geometric nonexistence certificates.

A separate free-incidence MILP at n=12 and n=16 reached its 30-second limit
without an incumbent. No size exclusion or near-counterexample is inferred.

## Smallest missing implications

1. Universal proof: force a certified obstruction, or a closed strict descent,
   from an arbitrary all-rich polygon. Two-row forbidden types do not suffice.
2. Angle approach: positive abstract chord directions need not integrate into
   planar points. An angle survivor is a preflight input, not a realization.
3. Construction: find a nonempty compatible convex subset with every retained
   vertex rich, then certify all equalities and every global support sign.
4. Minimality: deleting a vertex concerns **all actual distance classes**;
   uniqueness and size four are conclusions about fragile centers only.
