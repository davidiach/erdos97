   below `1/3` has a good point. The endpoint circle bound is at most three
   distinct intersections at every positive radius. This obstructs arbitrary
   on-carrier finite gluing, not only the original recurrence.

   common center from its source-side matching hypothesis and strict convexity.
   All 30 opposite-orientation cyclic orders have exact rational certificates.

   realized in strict convex position. The written proof no longer assumes
   equal triangle orientations or a collision-free deformation component.

The human geometric reductions and mixed-half-plane diamond proof remain
review obligations. Exact formula/certificate checks are supporting evidence,
not a formal proof checker for every written argument.

## Exact controls

counterexample. The 27-point control is exactly all-rich but has nine strict
interior points. Their independent-from-search coordinate checker uses
rational algebra and outward square-root enclosures, not floating-point hull
classification. The new 27-point output was generated during publication,
not retroactively attributed to the source research session.

## Exploratory boundary

near-collisions and incomplete hulls, not certified solutions. The 100 stored
off-carrier cap samples also have incomplete hulls when all returned suppliers
are included. Neither scan exhausts its search space. In particular the cap

repair gadgets. Numerical SVD ranks are not rank certificates.

## Missing implication

No argument forces an arbitrary all-rich strictly convex polygon onto the
six-arc carrier or into the fixed matched-equilateral/product witness system.
Neither an unrestricted reduction nor an exactly certified off-carrier,
changed-witness construction has been obtained. The original problem is not
resolved by these results. No new global status claim is inferred from their
publication.
